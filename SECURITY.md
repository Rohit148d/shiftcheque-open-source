# Security policy

Please use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting if available, or
contact maintainers privately through their public profile. Do not file
public issues containing exploitable details, real payroll data, or credentials.

**Financial and legal limitation:** ShiftCheque is an experimental planning
application. It is not payroll, tax, accounting, or legal advice; calculations
may be inaccurate or outdated. Verify decisions against the relevant government
rules and qualified professionals.

Security updates generally target the latest maintained release. Use
synthetic sample data in reports and tests.
